Car Mode is a touch-friendly dashboard for checking on and talking to your agents from a car browser. It is made for the Tesla in-car browser, and also works on tablets and other screens where Paseo's normal buttons are too small. Not affiliated with Tesla.

Open it from the Car mode row in the sidebar or from the Command Center. It shows the agents on the selected host as large cards. Agents waiting on a permission or question come first, then agents that just finished or failed, then working agents, then the rest. Paseo's workspace names are the card titles. Helper agents are folded into the card of the agent that started them. Large buttons along the top filter by project, and the Projects button turns projects on or off for this device. New workspace shows a large tile for each visible project and starts a new agent there from a typed or spoken first message, using the same model as your latest agent. On web, a small button hides and shows Paseo's sidebar. Closed agents untouched for three days are hidden behind a "Show older agents" button.

Tap a card to open the agent in car mode. A bar pinned to the top has buttons to go back, read the latest reply aloud, and open the normal Paseo agent screen. A bar pinned to the bottom holds the message box with Speak and Send buttons, so you can reply from anywhere in the conversation. In between are the agent's replies since your last message, big Allow and Deny buttons for any pending permission request, its background work with Stop buttons, its helper agents, and quick replies such as "Continue" and "Yes, go ahead".

Speak records from the browser's microphone and transcribes it with the speech engine Paseo uses on your computer, the same one behind Paseo's own voice typing. The audio goes to your Paseo daemon and nowhere else. Listen reads replies aloud with the Kokoro voice that Paseo's voice mode uses, generated on your computer and played on the device showing car mode. It skips code blocks and links. The voice model uses a few hundred MB of memory while loaded and unloads after five idle minutes. Kokoro must be installed in Paseo's voice settings. Speak and Listen need a daemon without a password. Otherwise the plugin uses Paseo's own connection and sends nothing anywhere else.
